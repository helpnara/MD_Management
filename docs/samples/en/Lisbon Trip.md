# Lisbon Trip

First week of October, three days. **Staying near the river.**

![](assets/river.jpg)

## Day by day

| Day | Where | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Alfama | Late arrival, dinner only |
| Day 2 | Belém | Start early |
| Day 3 | Sintra | Café if it rains |

## To pack

- A light jacket — the wind off the water is colder than it looks
- A rain shell instead of an umbrella
- One paper map

## To eat

1. Grilled sardines
2. Custard tarts
3. Green soup
