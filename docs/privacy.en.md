# Privacy Policy

Slow Margin **does not collect any personal information.**

## What we don’t collect

- We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or account details. There is no sign-up.
- We don’t collect usage data, analytics, or advertising identifiers.
- Your notes and photos are never sent to the developer or any third-party server. **There is no server.**

## Where your data lives

- Your `.md` files and attachments are stored **only on your device and in your own iCloud Drive.**
  iCloud sync is provided by Apple, and the developer can’t see its contents.
- The search index that makes searching fast stays **on your device only**, and you can delete it anytime
  (Settings → Rebuild Index).
- Deleting the app also deletes its search index. **Your files stay where they are.**
- When you send something to Slow Margin from another app’s share menu, it waits on your device until
  you open the app, then becomes a note in your folder.

## Permissions

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Photos | Adding images from your photo library to a note. Only the photos you choose are read |
| Camera | Taking a photo to add to a note |

You can decline either permission and still use everything else.
